## About the Challenge

**Challenge: Abandoned but not Forgotten — Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars**

Space exploration has left behind machines, landers, rovers, and exploration vehicles on the Moon and Mars. Although many of these missions have ended, their contributions to science and planetary exploration remain significant.

This challenge focuses on bringing attention to these forgotten pieces of space exploration history through engaging storytelling and accessible digital experiences.

## About Odyssey & Orbit

Odyssey & Orbit is a team passionate about turning space science into stories people can connect with.

For the NASA Space Apps Challenge, we aim to bring the forgotten stories of NASA's missions to life through an interactive web experience, combining NASA data, imagery, and human-centered storytelling.

Our project explores the journeys of robotic pioneers and historic exploration vehicles, showing what they were built to discover, what they experienced, and how their contributions helped expand humanity's understanding of other worlds.

## Our Approach

We transform forgotten mission hardware into engaging stories by:

- Exploring the history and purpose of NASA's missions.
- Presenting exploration journeys through interactive storytelling.
- Connecting scientific discoveries with human-centered narratives.
- Making space exploration history accessible and engaging.

## Worlds & Missions

Our experience takes visitors across two destinations:

### 🌕 The Moon

- **Surveyor 3** — An uncrewed lunar lander that explored the lunar surface and contributed valuable information about the Moon.
- **Apollo 17 Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV)** — The historic lunar rover that helped astronauts explore a wider area of the Moon during the Apollo 17 mission.

### 🔴 Mars

- **Opportunity Rover** — NASA's Mars Exploration Rover that exceeded its planned mission duration and made significant discoveries about the Red Planet.
- **Ingenuity Mars Helicopter** — The experimental helicopter that demonstrated powered, controlled flight on another planet.

Each explorer has a story highlighting its mission, discoveries, challenges, and lasting contribution to space exploration.
